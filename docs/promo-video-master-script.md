# WEAPONS.AI — "EVERY WEAPON, DECODED."
## Master 7-Minute Film Plan · Modular Production Bible (v2)
**Runtime:** 7:00 · **Aspect:** 16:9 · **Build method:** 7 independent modules, produced separately, assembled at the end.

---

## HOW THIS PLAN WORKS — THE MODULE SYSTEM

A 7-minute cinematic film is too much to make "in one go" — so you don't. You build **7 self-contained modules**. Each module has its own script, its own shots, its own tools, and can be finished on its own day. When all 7 exist, assembly is a half-day edit. If any one module fails or looks weak, you replace *that module* without touching the rest.

| # | Module | Runtime | Slot in film | Difficulty | Tools | Build time |
|---|--------|---------|--------------|------------|-------|-----------|
| M1 | Forge → Battlefield prologue | 1:00 | 0:00–1:00 | ★★★★ | AI video (Veo/Runway/Kling) | 1–2 days |
| M2 | Students' struggle → The Merge | 0:50 | 1:00–1:50 | ★★★ | Phone/camera + AI shot | 1 evening |
| M3 | Demo: The Command Library (AK-47, tanks, jets) | 1:05 | 1:50–2:55 | ★ | OBS screen capture | 2 hours |
| M4 | Demo: Full AI Research & Intelligence | 1:15 | 2:55–4:10 | ★ | OBS screen capture | 2 hours |
| M5 | Demo: Field News — live defense intelligence | 0:40 | 4:10–4:50 | ★ | OBS screen capture | 1 hour |
| M6 | The Engine Room: Tech Stack / API page + build war stories | 1:10 | 4:50–6:00 | ★★ | OBS + screenshots | half day |
| M7 | Resolution + Vision + CTA | 1:00 | 6:00–7:00 | ★★ | Camera + logo animation | 1 evening |

**Recommended build order (easiest first, momentum matters):**
M3 → M4 → M5 → M6 (all screen captures — you can finish ALL demos in one weekend) → VO recording (whole script in one session) → M2 → M7 (one filming evening covers both — same location, same actors) → M1 last (AI video needs iteration; do it when everything else is locked).

**Plan B — if even the modular build stalls:** ship it as a 3-part series instead. Part 1 = M1+M2+M3 ("The Reveal", 2:55), Part 2 = M4+M5 ("The Intelligence", 1:55 + extended cuts), Part 3 = M6+M7 ("How We Built It", 2:10). Same footage, three uploads, three chances at the algorithm. But the single 7:00 film is the stronger flagship — try that first.

**Retention rules baked into the structure:**
- Pattern break every ~30s (style change, silence beat, hard cut to UI).
- First "wow" (the Merge) before 1:50; first live product before 2:00.
- Demos are stories, not tours: every feature = *problem → live action → payoff line*.
- Total VO ≈ 940 words → ~135 wpm, with music-only gaps. Never rush the read.

### How to build it
Every shot is AI-generated video (Veo / Runway Gen-4 / Kling). Generate each shot at 3–5s, **3 takes each, keep the best**. Prompts:

1. *"Cinematic macro shot, molten steel pouring into a weapon mold inside a dark forge, sparks flying toward camera in slow motion, anamorphic lens flare, shallow depth of field, dark teal and orange color grade, photorealistic, 35mm film grain"*
2. *"Rifled gun barrel rotating on an industrial lathe, oil sheen, spiral grooves catching a single hard light, extreme close-up, slow push-in, dark industrial, photorealistic, cinematic"*
3. *"Slow dolly across a dark military assembly line, robotic arms assembling a rifle, blue welding flashes strobing, volumetric smoke, high contrast, photorealistic"*
4. *"Dawn battlefield, soldiers silhouetted against orange haze walking toward camera in slow motion, dust and embers in the air, epic wide, long lens compression, cinematic war film"*
5. *"Tank firing at dawn, massive muzzle flash, shockwave rippling dust outward, ultra slow motion, debris frozen mid-air, photorealistic, cinematic"*
6. *"Military helicopter passing low overhead at dawn, rotor wash flattening tall grass, low angle, lens flare, cinematic"*
7. *"Battlefield explosion frozen in time, colors draining to monochrome blueprint blue, scene dissolving into glowing wireframe mesh, VFX transition, cinematic"*

**Consistency trick (non-negotiable):** one dark teal/orange LUT + uniform 35mm grain + 2.35:1 letterbox bars over ALL AI clips, so mismatched generations read as one film. The blueprint-line shot (0:23) is a motion-graphics element (After Effects / Canva stroke-draw / an AI motion template) — reuse this exact element in M7's logo.

**Music:** dark hybrid-orchestral trailer cue (search: *"hybrid trailer", "military tension", "percussive rise"* on Artlist/Epidemic/Uppbeat). Structure: pulse → build → HIT at the tank shot → strip-back at the freeze.

If compositing is too heavy, use the AI shot alone, graded to match.

# MODULE 3 — DEMO: THE COMMAND LIBRARY (1:50 – 2:55)

> First live product. The star assets ONLY — AK-47, a tank, a jet. No generator cards, no pipelines: the message is *"an arsenal, ready, in 3D."* This module contains the film's most shareable 5 seconds: the AK-47 filling the screen, slowly rotating.

**Screen actions to record (OBS, 1080p60, clean browser, cursor hidden except when clicking):**
1. The Library grid — slow, steady scroll across the 30 asset cards. Let the grid breathe for 3s.
2. Click **AK-47** → the viewer opens → slow orbit → zoom to the receiver, then the muzzle. *(Record 3 takes; keep the smoothest orbit. Orbit SLOWLY — fast mouse movement reads amateur.)*
3. Click a **tank** (T-72 demos well) → orbit low-to-high like a crane shot → zoom the turret.
4. Click the **jet fighter** → one dramatic underside pass → pull out to full frame.

>
> **L10 (as the AK-47 opens):** "This is the AK-47. Not a photo. Not a diagram. The actual geometry — every curve of the receiver, every cut of the muzzle — sitting in your browser, waiting to be turned in your hands."
>
> **L11 (tank → jet montage):** "From the most produced rifle in history… to sixty tons of armor… to the fighters that own the sky. Click. Explore. Get closer than any textbook will ever let you."

# MODULE 4 — DEMO: FULL AI RESEARCH & INTELLIGENCE (2:55 – 4:10)

> The heart of the product story: the app doesn't just *show* weapons — it *researches* them. This is where the students' problem from M2 gets solved on screen.

**Screen actions to record:**
1. With the AK-47 open, click **Analyze**. Capture the "[ AWAITING INTELLIGENCE LINK ]" empty state first — then the analysis kicking off. (Speed-ramp the wait 10–20×, honest timer overlay in the corner.)

3. **Click-to-focus:** click 3 parts in the sidebar — camera flies to each part, mesh highlights with edge outline. (Barrel → magazine → trigger group. Rehearse the clicks so it looks choreographed.)
4. **Research tab:** scroll the four intelligence sections — *History & Evolution → Materials & Construction → How It Works → Key Information*. Pause 2s on each heading; punch-zoom one strong sentence per section.
5. Flash the color-coded part categories / risk view for 2s — enough to register depth without explaining it.

# THE VOICE — CASTING & DIRECTION (this decides "human vs. AI" more than anything)

**Character:** a 38-year-old documentary narrator — *Netflix military doc meets senior engineer*. Warm chest register, unhurried, quietly confident. Not selling — *letting you in on something*. In M6 he drops half a register and gets conversational: that's the founder, not the narrator.

**Human recording (strongly preferred):** 6 inches off-axis from the mic, blanket-treated room, three full takes — neutral / 10% more intimate / 10% more epic — comp the best lines.

**AI voice (ElevenLabs etc.) — the 7 rules that make it pass as human:**
1. Generate **per-module**, never the whole script in one call — vary delivery settings per module (M1: slow/low · M3–M5: brighter, quicker · M6: conversational, lower · M7: warm).
2. Keep the contractions and the "…honestly?" — formal grammar is the #1 AI tell.
3. The em-dashes and *(beats)* are load-bearing: enforce the pauses in the edit even if the TTS ignores them. Silence = humanity.
4. Add a real **breath sound** (freesound.org, −20dB) before long sentences. Single highest-impact trick.
5. Punch the 3-word sentences ("Drawn. Machined. Perfected."), glide the long ones.
6. De-ess, gentle saturation, real room reverb at 5–8% wet — sterile dryness reads synthetic.
7. Master VO to **−16 LUFS**; sidechain-duck music to −28 under speech.

# EDIT & SOUND BIBLE

- **One grade to rule them all:** dark teal/orange LUT on cinema modules; on UI captures, lift the app's blueprint blues and add 4% grain + the same letterbox so screen recordings live in the same world as the film.
- **Cut rhythm:** M1–M2 cut ON music hits · M3–M5 cut on UI events (click, model-appears, tab-change) · never let a static screen sit >3s without motion (punch-zoom, pan, highlight).
- **SFX are 50% of "premium":** every UI click = tactile tick · model-materialize = reverse-swell into soft boom · annotations landing = soft radar pings · news items = typewriter ticks · the Merge = building paper-flutter into the HIT.
- **Speed-ramps:** all waiting compressed 10–20× with an honest timer overlay ("Analysis: 90 seconds, real time") — honesty builds trust, hidden waits destroy it.
- **Captions:** burn in the 6 key lines (L4, L8, L14, L15's last sentence, L19's opener, L25). 40% of viewers are muted.
- **Loudness targets:** VO −16 LUFS · music bed −28 under VO, −18 in music-only sections · true peak −1.5 dB.
